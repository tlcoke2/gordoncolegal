Family Law in London

Family difficulties often involve several connected decisions. Gordon & Co Legal Services is based in London and helps clients with family law matters involving children, care proceedings and protection from domestic abuse.

Choosing the right support

Tell us what has happened, who is involved and whether there is a hearing or deadline. Our team can discuss the services relevant to your circumstances, the information needed and the next steps before you decide whether to instruct us.

Children and family disputes

Our priority areas include private children arrangements, public law children and care proceedings, and domestic abuse protection. The pages below explain how to contact us about each area. If your enquiry involves more than one issue, mention that when you get in touch.

Clients in the UK and Europe

We welcome enquiries from clients in the UK and clients based in Europe with a family matter connected to the UK. Please tell us where everyone lives and which country is handling any proceedings so that we can confirm whether the firm can assist. This is not a claim to practise the domestic law of every European country.

What to include in your first enquiry

  • A brief summary of the issue and the help you need
  • Any court date, deadline or existing order
  • The countries in which the people involved live
