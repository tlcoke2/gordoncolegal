Public Law Children & Care Proceedings in London

If social services are involved with your family, understanding the concerns and the next steps matters. Gordon & Co Legal Services supports enquiries about public law children matters and care proceedings from its Lewisham, London office.

Talk to our team ↗

When to contact the team

Contact us if you have received a letter about proposed proceedings, court papers or notice of a hearing involving your child. Tell us the date of any meeting or hearing at the start of your enquiry so that the team can assess availability and the assistance required.

How we can help

Our existing practice includes local authority intervention, care and supervision proceedings, safeguarding issues and preparation of evidence. We can discuss the documents involved, your concerns and the representation you are seeking. The services available will depend on the case and the agreed instructions.

Preparing an enquiry

A short timeline can help explain what has happened. Note which local authority is involved, who has contacted you and whether any court orders are already in place. Ask the team how to provide sensitive documents securely before sending them.

What to include in your first enquiry

  • Letters from the local authority and any court papers
  • The next hearing or meeting date
  • Details of any current legal representative
