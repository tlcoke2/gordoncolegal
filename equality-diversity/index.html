Equality & Diversity

Gordon & Co is committed to treating clients, colleagues and everyone who contacts us with dignity and respect. Our services are for people of all genders and backgrounds.

Fair and respectful treatment

We aim to provide an environment free from discrimination, harassment and victimisation. We recognise that people bring different experiences, needs and perspectives, and we approach each person’s circumstances individually.

Accessible communication

Please tell us about any disability-related adjustments, communication needs or other support that would help you use our services. You can ask about alternative document formats, accessible appointments or language support. We will discuss practical arrangements with you.

Raising a concern

If you have concerns about how you have been treated, contact info@gordoncolegal.com. You can also use our complaints procedure.
