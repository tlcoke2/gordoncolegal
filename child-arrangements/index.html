Child Arrangements Legal Support in London

Decisions about where children live and spend time can be difficult after separation. Gordon & Co Legal Services helps clients seeking support with private children arrangements, with the child’s welfare at the centre of the discussion.

Talk to our team ↗

Private children arrangements

Our work includes enquiries about child arrangements and disagreements concerning a child’s upbringing. Tell us the arrangements currently in place, what needs to change and whether there is an existing court order. This helps us understand the practical issue you want to resolve.

Specific issues and proposed changes

The firm also handles enquiries involving specific issue and prohibited steps orders. If the disagreement concerns schooling, travel or another important decision, explain the proposed action and any deadline. We will discuss the relevant service and information needed for your circumstances.

Safety and international connections

Mention any safety concerns when you first contact us. If a child or parent lives abroad, tell us which countries are involved and whether proceedings have already started. We welcome enquiries from clients in Europe and will confirm whether we can assist with the matter.

What to include in your first enquiry

  • Existing orders or written arrangements
  • A brief explanation of the disagreement
  • Any deadlines, travel plans or safety concerns
