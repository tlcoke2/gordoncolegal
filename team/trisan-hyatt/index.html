Background

A highly sought-after Legal 500 leading junior, Trisan is forensic and meticulous in case preparation. Her background in teaching honed clear communication and courtroom control.

She accepts instructions across the full range of family law: private children, care proceedings, domestic abuse, Female Genital Mutilation Protection Orders, forced marriage, and occupation order proceedings, with a particular interest in cross family–immigration issues.

She has significant experience before healthcare regulators (GDC, NMC) and in appellate courts.

Teaching and professional development

Alongside her practice, Trisan teaches at the University of Westminster Law School. She supervises pupils and delivers equality and diversity training at Gray’s Inn. Her earlier teaching work covered sociology and psychology.

Education

Her qualifications include a BA (Hons) from the University of the West Indies, an LLM from Queen Mary College, University of London, and a Diploma in Teacher Education.

Professional memberships

Trisan belongs to the Family Law Bar Association, Young Legal Aid Lawyers and the Discrimination Law Association.
