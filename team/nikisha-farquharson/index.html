Background

Nikisha specialises in Family Law, known for meticulous attention to detail, strategic insight and unwavering client support.

Her practice covers divorce, financial remedies, Children Act proceedings, care matters and domestic abuse cases, including urgent injunctive relief.

She prepares and issues proceedings, drafts tailored statements and instructions to counsel/experts, and provides pragmatic advice throughout.

Prior to law, she worked within a London Local Authority supporting safeguarding processes and also taught History, strengthening analytical and communication skills.
