Domestic Abuse Legal Support in Lewisham, London

Seeking legal help about domestic abuse can be difficult. Gordon & Co Legal Services offers sensitive support with protective family-law matters, including enquiries about non-molestation and occupation orders.

Talk to our team ↗

Discussing protection

A non-molestation order can protect against harm or threats. An occupation order concerns who can live in or enter the family home. Contact the team to discuss your circumstances and the legal assistance you are seeking; the appropriate route depends on the facts of your case.

A safe way to contact you

When making an enquiry, tell us which email address or contact method is safe to use and whether messages can be left. You do not need to include a full account of sensitive events in your initial email. Ask how to provide documents securely.

Children and urgent dates

Please mention if children are affected, if proceedings are already underway or if there is a court deadline. The team can consider the relationship between protective matters and children arrangements when discussing your enquiry. Contacting the website does not guarantee an immediate response.

What to include in your first enquiry

  • A safe way for the team to contact you
  • Any existing court orders or hearing dates
  • A brief description of the legal help needed
